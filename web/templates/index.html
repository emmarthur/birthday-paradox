About this app

In a group of people, the chance that at least two share a birthday is higher than most people expect. This app picks random calendar dates for each person, then repeats many trials to estimate the probability.

How to use it

  • Set the group size (try 23 — often near a 50% match rate).
  • Generate one sample shows a single random group and whether any birthday repeats.
  • Run {{ simulation_count | int }} simulations repeats the experiment many times and shows an estimated percentage (progress updates every 10,000 runs).
